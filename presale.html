QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Presale information.

What is Campus Money?

Campus Money is a digital currency project being developed for utility and ecosystem access across the Zep Swipe African student economy.

Which blockchain will Campus Money use?

Campus Money is planned for the Base blockchain. The official token contract address will only be published after deployment and verification.

Does holding Campus Money give me company ownership?

No. The proposed holder rights are limited to utility and ecosystem access. Campus Money does not represent shares, equity, voting rights or ownership in Zep Swipe or another company.

Does Campus Money guarantee financial returns?

No. There are no promised profits, appreciation, dividends, buybacks or guaranteed resale opportunities.

How do the KOVÉR watch rewards work?

The proposed programme uses cumulative qualifying contribution thresholds. A participant would qualify for the highest eligible tier achieved, subject to final reward terms. Watch production, specifications and fulfilment arrangements must be confirmed before rewards are offered.

Can I buy Campus Money right now?

This page does not currently accept payments or token purchases. Official participation instructions will be published only after the necessary legal and technical preparations are complete.

Is Campus Money legal tender?

No. Campus Money is not presented as legal tender or as currency issued or guaranteed by a central bank.
