How referral commissions work

1. A customer visits KOVÉR using your tracked referral link.
2. The customer completes a qualifying purchase.
3. The payment provider confirms the transaction.
4. The commission is recorded as pending.
5. Once the watch is delivered and applicable refund, cancellation and chargeback checks are complete, the commission may become available.
6. Withdrawals follow the published Guild payout rules.
