Beyond a digital currency

Building an economy around student opportunity.

Campus Money is the proposed digital utility currency at the centre of a broader student economy ecosystem.

The vision is to connect students with businesses, services, mobility solutions, accommodation, skills, entrepreneurship and partnerships.

Rather than treating student life as a temporary stage, we see universities and colleges as powerful starting points for economic participation and long-term opportunity.

The development journey

Building the ecosystem
step by step.

The ecosystem is being developed progressively. The following areas represent its intended direction, not a promise that every feature is already live.

PHASE 01

Foundation

Establish the Campus Money identity, wallet-first account experience, ecosystem information and core technical infrastructure.

PHASE 02

Student economy connections

Develop connections to student services, businesses, opportunities and relevant partner initiatives.

PHASE 03

Digital utility integration

Introduce compatible Campus Money utility experiences after technical, security and regulatory readiness.

PHASE 04

Expansion

Explore wider university, college, business and institutional collaborations across African markets.
