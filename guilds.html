WHY THE GUILD EXISTS

The future belongs to those who participate.

The Campus Money Guild is a community designed to connect students with opportunities to learn, contribute, collaborate and grow.

We believe students have more to offer than their qualifications. They have creativity, influence, skills, ideas and the ability to build meaningful connections.

The Guild creates a structured environment where members can take on responsibilities, support commercial initiatives and become active participants in a growing student economy.

CONTRIBUTION MATTERS

Recognition for the value you create.

The Guild is built around meaningful participation. Members may qualify for rewards when they complete approved work and achieve verified results.

Verified Referral Rewards Eligible commissions for qualifying completed sales.
Approved Contribution Payments Payments for agreed work and accepted deliverables.
Points & Recognition Build your Guild standing through participation and achievements.
Community Privileges Access eligible activities, recognition and selected opportunities.

Your contribution is your reputation.

The Campus Money Guild recognises effort, creativity, responsibility and measurable contribution. Members can build a record of their activities and eligible achievements.

Rewards are subject to role requirements, availability, approval and verification. Membership does not guarantee income, commissions or employment. Rewards are not paid simply for joining or recruiting other members.
