INTRODUCING CAMPUS MONEY

More than a digital currency. A student economy.

Across Africa, millions of students have skills, ambition and ideas. Yet many lack access to the opportunities, business networks and economic participation they need before graduation.

Campus Money is being developed as a utility layer for the Zep Swipe student economy, with the long-term goal of connecting students, businesses, partners and communities through useful digital services.

Our vision is to make the student economy more connected, accessible and opportunity-driven.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Get to know Campus Money.

What is Campus Money?

Campus Money is a digital currency project being developed for the Zep Swipe African student economy. Its intended role is utility and ecosystem access, subject to the final product design and applicable law.

How is Campus Money connected to Zep Swipe?

Zep Swipe is building a platform that connects students, businesses, partners and opportunities. Campus Money is being developed as a digital utility component within that broader vision.

How many Campus Money tokens are proposed?

The proposed maximum supply is one billion tokens. Refer to the token documentation for the allocation, controls and any final changes.

Can I connect my wallet?

Wallet connection and purchasing will be made available only through the supported purchase process once the offering, technical infrastructure and applicable legal requirements are ready.

How do I qualify for a KOVÉR watch?

The proposed programme uses cumulative qualifying contributions to determine eligibility. Verified participants will be able to complete a watch claim form once the final programme rules are published.

Does Campus Money represent ownership in Zep Swipe?

No. The intended holder rights are utility and ecosystem access only. Campus Money does not represent equity or ownership in Zep Swipe or another company.
