Campus Money Guild payout policy

• Minimum withdrawal: $200 in available rewards.
• Pending rewards cannot be withdrawn.
• KOVÉR referral commissions remain pending until delivery and applicable refund or chargeback checks are complete.
• Approved non-sales mission payments become available after the agreed work has been completed and approved.
• Payout requests are subject to verification and published processing times.
• Network fees, if applicable, must be disclosed before a payout is confirmed.
